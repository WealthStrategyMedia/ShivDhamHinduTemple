## Translation

*Gajaananam Bhoota Ganaadhi Sevitam*  
*Kapitha Jamboo Phaala Saara Bhakshitam*  
*Umaasutam Shoka Vinaasha Kaaranam*  
*Namaami Vighneswara Paada Pankajam*

The Lord with the elephant face, served by all the Ganas, one who takes as His food the essence of Kapitta and Jamboophala (two favorite fruits of Ganesh), son of Uma (Mother Parvati), destroyer of misery, controller of obstacles: we worship Your lotus feet.
