## Translation

*šri gãyatri jaganmãtã parabrahma svarupini |*  
*paramãrtha pradã japyã brahmatejo vivardhini ||*

O Shri Gayatri Devi, mother of the universe, the supreme being, a true bestower and generous presence, who carries away the weight of our sins. I bow to you and offer my prayers.
