## Translation

*mãtã cha pãrvati devi pitã devo mahešwara: |*  
*bãndhavã: šiva bhaktãcha svadésho bhuvanatryam ||*

Goddess Parvati is the divine mother, Lord Shiva the divine father, and fellow devotees are the relatives. The three worlds are created by them, and we all reside there together.
