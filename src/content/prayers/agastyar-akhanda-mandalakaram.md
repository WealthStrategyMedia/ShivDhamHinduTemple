## Translation

*akhanda-mandalãkãram vyãptam yena charãcharam*  
*tatpadam daršitam yenã tasmai śri-guravé namaha*

Salutations to that Guru who showed me the way, who helped me realize the truth, whose form pervades the universe, present in all that is movable and immovable.
