## Translation

*vasudéva-sutam dévam kamsa-chãnoora-mardanam |*  
*dévaki-paramãnandam krishnam vandé jagad-gurum ||*

I salute Lord Krishna, the teacher of the world, son of Vasudeva, destroyer of Kamsa and Chanoora, and the greatest joy of Devaki, his mother.
