लोके जरा रुग् भय मृत्यु नाशकं

</div>

## Translation

*namãmi dhanvantarim ãdi devam*  
*surãsura vanditam pãda padmam*  
*loké jarã rug bhaya mrityu nãšakam dãthãram eshãm vividha aushadhinãm*

I pray to Lord Dhanvantari, my primary Lord, whose lotus feet are worshiped by both the celestial and worldly beings, and who, through the many medicines he gives, removes the fear of disease, old age, and death.
