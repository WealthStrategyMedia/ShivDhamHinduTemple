## Translation

*swarnãkarshana bhairavam devam sarva šakti saman-vitham |*  
*sarvãbheeshtha balam dehi šri kshetrapãla namostuté ||*

Salutations to Lord Swarnakarshana Bhairavar, who is the source of all energy and power. Give us your grace to fulfill all our wishes, you who guard the temple.
