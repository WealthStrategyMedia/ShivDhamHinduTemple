## Translation

*sahasrãditya sankãšam*  
*sahasra vadanam prabhum*  
*sahasrãdam sahasrãram*  
*prapadhyé(a)ham sudaršanam*

I unconditionally surrender at the feet of Lord Sudarshana, who shines with the brilliance of a thousand suns from a thousand faces, with a wheel-like form of a thousand spokes.
