## Translation

*sarva mangala mãngalye šive sarvãrtha sãdaké |*  
*šaranyé tryambaké gowri nãrãyani namostuté ||*

Salutations to you, O Devi, who is the auspiciousness of all that is auspicious, who is the consort of Lord Shiva, who is the means of accomplishing all desires, who is the refuge of all, who is three-eyed, and who is fair-complexioned.
