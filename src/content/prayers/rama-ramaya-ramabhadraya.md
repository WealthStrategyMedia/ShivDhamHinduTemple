## Translation

*rãmãya rãma-bhadrãya rãma-chandrãya védhasé |*  
*raghunãthãya nãthãya seethãyãḥ patayé namah  ||*

Salutations to Lord Rama, who is auspiciousness itself, who is present as a shining light within all beings, who is the creator, the Lord of the Raghu clan, the Lord of all beings, and the husband of Sita.
