## Translation

*namasté astu bhagavan višvéswarãya, mahãdévãya, tryambakãya,*  
*tripurantakãya, trikãlãgni-kãlãya, kãlãgni-rudrãya, neela-kantãya,*  
*mrityun-jayãya, sarvešvarãya, sadã-šivãya šriman mahã-dévãya namaha ||*

Salutations to you, O Bhagavan (Lord) Vishweshwara, Mahadeva (Great God), who is known as Trayambaka (three-eyed), Tripurantaka (destroyer of the three cities), who is the Lord of the three times (past, present, and future), the fire of the final dissolution, who is Rudra in the form of Time, the blue-throated one, who is the conqueror of death (Mrityunjaya), who is the Lord of all (Sarveshwara), eternal auspiciousness (Sadasiva). Obeisance to you, O glorious Mahadeva.
