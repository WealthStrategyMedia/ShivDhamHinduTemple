## Translation

*saraswati namastubhyam varadé kãma-roopini*  
*vidhyãrambham karishyãmi siddhir-bhavatu mé sadã*

Salutations to you, O Goddess Saraswati, who is the giver of boons and has a beautiful form. I begin my studies; let there always be success and true understanding for me.
