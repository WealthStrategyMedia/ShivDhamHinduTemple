## Translation

*manojavam māruta-tulya-végam*  
*jiténdriyam buddhi-matām varishṭham*  
*vātātmajam vānara-yootha-mukhyam*  
*śri-rāma-dhootham śirasā namāmi*

I bow my head to and salute Lord Hanuman, who can think as fast as the wind, who has mastered his senses, who is the best among the wise, the son of the wind god, the commander of the monkey army, and the messenger of Lord Rama.
