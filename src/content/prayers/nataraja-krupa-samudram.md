## Translation

*krupā samudhram sumukham trinethram jatādharam pārvati vāmabhāgam |*  
*sadašivam rudramanantharoopam šivachidambarešam hrudhibhāvayāmi ||*

Salutations to the Lord of Chidambaram, who is an ocean of compassion, has a pleasant face, three eyes, matted hair, and Goddess Parvati at his left side. He is always peaceful, fierce, and boundless in form.
