## Translation

*kunkumãngitavarnãya*  
*kundendudhavalãya cha*  
*vishnuvãha namastubhyam*  
*pakshi rãjãya té namaha*

Salutations to the king of birds and the vehicle of Vishnu, Garuda, who is red-colored like vermilion and white like jasmine and the moon.
