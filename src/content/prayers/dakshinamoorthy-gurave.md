## Translation

*gurave sarva-lokãnãm bhišaje bhava-roginãm*  
*nidhaye sarva-vidhyãnãm dakshinãmoortaye namaha*

Salutations to Lord Dakshinamoorthy, who is the teacher of all the worlds, the remover of all sorrows born of limitation, and the abode of all learning.
