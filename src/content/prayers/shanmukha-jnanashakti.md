## Translation

*gñãnašaktidhara skanda valli kalyãna sundara*  
*devasenã mana: kãnta kãrtikeya namostute*

Salutations to Lord Kartikeya, who is known as Skanda, holds the spear of wisdom, is the beloved of the beautiful Goddess Valli, and has captured the heart of Goddess Devasena.
