## Translation

*sarvasva roopé sarveše sarva-šakti-samanvité*  
*bhayébhya-strãhi no devi durgé devi namostu té*

My salutations to you, O Durga Devi, you who are the embodiment of all forms, the goddess of all, possessor of all energy. Kindly protect us from all fears.
