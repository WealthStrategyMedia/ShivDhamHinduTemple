## Translation

*Satyanãrãyanam devam vandeham kãmadam priyam*  
*leelayã vithatham višwam yena tasmai namo namaha*

I pray to Lord Satyanarayana to fulfill our wishes. I bow to the Lord who, through his many forms, can be seen everywhere in this world.
