ज्वलन्तं सर्वतॊ मुखम्।

</div>

## Translation

*ugram veeram mahã-vishnum*  
*jvalantam sarvato mukham*  
*nrusimham bheeshanam bhadram*  
*mrutyor mrutyum namãmyaham*

I bow to Lord Narasimha, a form of Maha Vishnu, who is ferocious, courageous, radiant on every side, awe-inspiring, and auspicious, and who overcomes the cycle of birth and death.
