## Translation

*namasté'stu mahãmãyé šrīpeeté surapoojité*  
*šankha-chakra-gadã-hasté Mahã Lakshmi namo'stu té*

Salutations to you, O Mahalakshmi, who is all power, is the seat of wealth, is worshiped by the gods, and holds a conch, a discus, and a mace in her hands.
