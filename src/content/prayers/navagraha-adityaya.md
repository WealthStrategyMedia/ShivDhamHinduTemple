## Translation

*ãdityãyacha-somãya mangalãya-budhãyacha |*  
*guru-šukra-šanibhyašcha rãhavé-kétavé namaha ||*

I bow to the Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, and to Rahu and Ketu.
