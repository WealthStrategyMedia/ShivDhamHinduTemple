## Translation

*poojyãya rãghavendrãya satya dharma rathãyacha |*  
*bhajathãm kalpa-vrukshãya namathãm kãma-dhenavé ||*

Salutations to Shri Raghavendra, who always walks the path of truth and righteousness. Like the wish-fulfilling Kalpavruksha tree and the wish-fulfilling Kamadhenu cow, he fulfills the wishes of those who pray and chant his name.
