## Translation

*kumbhacandašcaturbãhuh tankašulãsidandadhrik |*  
*raktavarno mahãdipto bhadrapithopari sthitah ||*

I pray to Shri Ganesha Kumbha Chandikeshwara, who is meditating, seated on a lotus base close to the shrine, holding an axe, spear, and stick, blazing in red color as a noble presence, to ensure I receive the blessings of Shri Maha Vallabha Ganapati through him, as a result of my Ganapati Pooja.
