## Translation

*ananto vãsuki: šeša: padmanãbhašcha kambala: šankhapãlo dhãrtarãštra: |*  
*takšaka: kãliyastathã ethãni navanãmãni nãgãnãm cha mahãtmãnam ||*

Salutations to Ananta, Vasuki, Shesha, Padmanabha, Kambala, Shankapala, Dhartarashtra, Takshaka, and Kaliya, the nine great serpents among all serpents.
