## Translation

*nilakantha patãṁboja padasphurita mãnasa |*  
*šivapujãphalam dehi dhvanicanda namostute ||*

I pray to Shri Shiva Dhvani Chandikeshwara, who is meditating, seated on a lotus base, holding a triumph, with a shining body and mind, to ensure I receive the blessings of Shri Shiva (known as Nilakantha) through him, as a result of my Shiva Pooja.
