Sadvidya Pathshala teaches sacred chants: shlokas, stotras, the Lalita Sahasranama, the Vishnu Sahasranama, and the Bhagavad Gita, with attention to correct pronunciation, for both children and adults. The goal is not just memorization but a deeper spiritual connection built through the practice of chanting itself.

Sign up beginning this Sunday or the upcoming Sunday using the registration form on the [temple's contact page](/contact-us/), noting whether you're enrolling as a child or an adult.
