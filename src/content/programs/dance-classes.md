Kalagangothri is the temple's classical dance program, teaching **Bharatanatyam** and **Kuchipudi** in one-hour weekly sessions that combine dance technique, music knowledge, and the theory behind each art form.

Classes are taught by Guru Akhila Chetan, Ms. Gauri, and Guru Chetan H, and meet Saturdays at 2:00 PM at the temple (schedule changes are announced by email in advance).

Registration is $50 (non-refundable), with season tuition of $300 for a 3-month season plus a $15 per-season temple donation, due at the start of each season.
