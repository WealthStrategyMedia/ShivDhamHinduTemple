The temple's summer camp gives children a five-day introduction to spiritual foundations through age-appropriate activities, chanting, and stories drawn from Hindu scripture. The temple believes that a grounding in spiritual values helps children build emotional resilience, empathy, and stronger family bonds alongside their academic growth.

## Highlights

- Vedic chanting, including Dasha Shanti mantras and the Guruparampara Stotram
- Recitation of Maitreem Bhajata and world peace prayers
- Stories and lessons on friendship and values drawn from the Bhagavad Gita
- Surya Namaskara and yoga asanas

Seats are limited each season. See the [registration form](/summer-camp-registration-form/) to enroll.
