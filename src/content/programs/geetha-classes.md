Geetha classes guide students through the teachings of the Bhagavad Gita, exploring its philosophy and its practical guidance for living a balanced, purposeful life. Sessions are open to students at any stage of their study of the text.

Contact the [temple office](/contact-us/) for the current schedule and enrollment details.
