Bal Vihar is the temple's children's program, introducing young devotees to Hindu stories, values, shlokas, and traditions in an engaging, age-appropriate setting. It's often a child's first regular connection to temple life and community.

Contact the [temple office](/contact-us/) for the current class schedule and enrollment details.
