Pandit Srivatsa brings deep knowledge of Vedic scripture, ritual, and mantra to every ceremony he performs at the temple, from weddings and regular pujas to major festival rituals. As a full-fledged Purohit, he is trained in the traditional Shodasha Karma life-cycle ceremonies, including Jatakarma (birth), Nama Karana (naming), the sacred thread ceremony, Vivaha (marriage), and Gruhapravesha (housewarming), as well as Shraddha ceremonies honoring ancestors.

His expertise extends to major Vedic rituals including Maha Kumbhabhisheka, Devatha Prathishtapana, Devatha Kalyanothsava, Rudra Homa, and Chandika Homa. Fluent in Sanskrit, Telugu, Tamil, and Hindi, he serves the temple's diverse congregation with warmth and precision.
