A graduate of Sadvidhya Sanjeevini Samskruta Maha Patashala, Pandit Suman brings over 15 years of experience in Vedic ritual and spiritual practice to the temple. He performs the full range of Hindu ceremonies, weddings, regular pujas, and festival rituals, with the same devotion and precision, and is a respected Purohit trained in the Shodasha Karma life-cycle rites, from Jatakarma and Namakarana through Vivaha and Gruhapravesha, as well as Shraddha ceremonies for ancestors.

His practice also includes major spiritual events such as Maha Kumbhabhisheka, Devatha Prathishtapana, Devatha Kalyanothsava, and Rudra Homa, and he draws on a deep grounding in the Upanishads and Bhagavad Gita to guide devotees in their spiritual growth. He is fluent in Sanskrit, Kannada, Telugu, and English.
