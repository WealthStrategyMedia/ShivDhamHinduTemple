Navaratri is celebrated over nine nights of dedicated worship of the divine mother, with daily puja, devotional music, and cultural programs bringing the community together. The festival builds toward Durga Puja and the celebration of the goddess's triumph over evil. See [Navratri Sponsorship](/navratri-sponsorship/) to support the celebration.
