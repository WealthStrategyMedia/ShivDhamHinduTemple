Satyanarayan Pooja is a worship of Lord Vishnu performed to express gratitude and seek continued blessings, frequently sponsored by families to mark a milestone or give thanks for a wish fulfilled. See the [Events Calendar](/events/) for upcoming dates, or arrange a sponsored pooja through [Puja Bookings](/puja-bookings/).
