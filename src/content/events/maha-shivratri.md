Maha Shivratri holds special significance at Shri Shiv Dham: the temple's grand opening was celebrated on this very festival in 2003. Devotees gather for a night of continuous worship, abhishekam, and devotional singing in honor of Lord Shiva. It is the temple's largest and most important annual celebration.
