Sankata Hara Chaturthi is observed monthly in honor of Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles. Devotees gather for evening puja seeking his blessings for relief from difficulties and for wisdom on the path ahead. Check the [Events Calendar](/events/) for this month's exact date, as it shifts with the lunar calendar.
