Pradosham falls twice each lunar month, in the twilight hours considered especially auspicious for worshipping Lord Shiva. The temple holds evening puja on each occasion, drawing devotees seeking Shiva's grace and blessings. See the [Events Calendar](/events/) for the next Pradosham date.
