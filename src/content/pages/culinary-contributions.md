Many of the temple's festival celebrations, from Annakoot Mahotsav's grand offering of 108 dishes to smaller community gatherings, are made possible by devotees who bring a homemade dish to share. Contributing a dish is a simple, meaningful way to take part in the celebration and help feed everyone who comes.

If you'd like to contribute a dish for an upcoming festival or event, or want to know what's needed for the next celebration, reach out to the temple's canteen team.

[Contact the temple office](/contact-us/) to offer a culinary contribution, or see the [Canteen](/canteen/) page to learn more about the team behind the temple's meals.
