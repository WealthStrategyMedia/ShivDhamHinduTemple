The temple observes the Hindu lunar calendar's tithis, ekadashis, sankashtis, and major festivals throughout the year. Dates are set by the traditional Panchang and may shift slightly by region. The calendar below reflects the dates observed at Shri Shiv Dham.

Schedules can occasionally change because of weather or other circumstances. For any updates, don't hesitate to [contact the temple office](/contact-us/) directly or call (+1) 407-380-2661.
