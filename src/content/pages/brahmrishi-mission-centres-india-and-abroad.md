Shri Shiv Dham Hindu Temple is a proud part of the **Brahmrishi Mission**, founded and led by H.H. Brahmrishi Vishvatma Bawra Ji Maharaj, Chief Founder and Spiritual Director of this internationally recognized mission. At Shri Shiv Dham, Gurudev's teachings are brought to life through religious, cultural, and social programs that offer a space for personal spiritual growth as well as community connection and service.

Guided by the Mission's core values, love, selfless service, and spiritual awakening, Shri Shiv Dham Hindu Temple invites everyone to experience the transformative power of faith, wisdom, and compassionate action.

## About the Brahmrishi Mission

The Brahmrishi Mission has grown into a global spiritual movement, with centers across India and around the world, all dedicated to spreading the light of knowledge and devotion. Founded by H.H. Brahmrishi Vishvatma Bawra Ji Maharaj, these centers offer spiritual teaching, educational institutions, yoga ashrams, and humanitarian projects, a testament to the Mission's commitment to empowering individuals and communities through divine wisdom and selfless service.
