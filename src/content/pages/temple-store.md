The temple store offers puja items, festival goods, and seasonal offerings for devotees, from Holi color for the spring festival to items tied to the temple's seasonal celebrations.

Many of the temple's puja and ceremony offerings are also available to book directly: see [Temple Services](/puja-bookings/) for the full list, including the [Holi Color Packet](/puja-bookings/1-packet-holi-color/).

For an item not listed online, [contact the temple office](/contact-us/) directly.
