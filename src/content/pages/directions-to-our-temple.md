## By Car

**From Downtown Orlando:** Head east on FL-50 E / E Colonial Dr, turn left onto Lake Pickett Rd, then left onto Oberry Hoover Rd. The temple is on the right.

**From I-4:** Take exit 82A to merge onto FL-408 E toward Titusville, then take exit 19 for Dean Rd. Turn left onto Dean Rd, right onto FL-50 E / E Colonial Dr, left onto Lake Pickett Rd, then left onto Oberry Hoover Rd. The temple is on the right.

## By Air

The nearest airport is **Orlando International Airport (MCO)**. Exit the airport following signs for FL-528 E toward Cocoa, take exit 16 for FL-551 N toward Goldenrod Rd, merge onto FL-551 N / N Goldenrod Rd, then turn right onto FL-50 E / E Colonial Dr. Turn left onto Lake Pickett Rd, then left onto Oberry Hoover Rd. The temple is on the right.

## By Public Transit

Take Lynx Bus Route 15 from downtown Orlando (or a connecting route) to the Lake Pickett Rd & Oberry Hoover Rd stop, then walk a short distance to the temple.

## Parking

The temple has its own on-site parking, though space is limited, so carpooling is appreciated on major festival days.
