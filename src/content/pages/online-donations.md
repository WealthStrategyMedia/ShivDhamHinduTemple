Every donation helps sustain daily worship, priest support, festival celebrations, and the community programs that make Shri Shiv Dham a home for Central Florida's Hindu community. Gifts of any size are deeply appreciated.

## Ways to Give

**Online, by card.** Secure online giving is available directly through this page.

**By Zelle.** Donations can also be sent via Zelle to +1 (407) 779-5128.

For any donation-related questions, call the temple office at +1 (407) 779-5128.

## Designated Funds

- **[General Fund](/donations/):** supports day-to-day temple operations and worship.
- **[Shivadham](/donations/shivadham/):** supports the temple's core mission and facilities.
- **[Temple Garden](/support-the-temple-garden/):** supports restoration of the temple garden.
