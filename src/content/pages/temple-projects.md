The temple is working toward an expansion project with three goals:

- Extending the prayer hall to accommodate more devotees and more space for prayer
- Adding new bathrooms and showers for devotees who come from work, or stay overnight for prayers and offerings
- Creating an administrative office with a books and library space, to support education and camps for children

As the temple takes this next step, the community's support makes the difference. The goal is to be better equipped for a larger congregation, with special emphasis on encouraging youth and young professionals to get involved in temple and community life.

Materials, labor, and the everyday costs of a project like this add up quickly, and every contribution helps carry it forward. All contributions are tax deductible.

May the blessings of Bhagwanji be always with you and your family.
