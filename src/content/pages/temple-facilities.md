The temple grounds include shrines for each of the seven enshrined deities, spaces for daily prayer and larger festival gatherings, a community center used for classes and events, and the temple [canteen](/canteen/) serving prasad and meals on puja days and festivals.

On-site parking is available, though limited. See [Directions](/directions-to-our-temple/) for details. For a look at the temple's spaces and celebrations, visit the [Gallery](/gallery/).
