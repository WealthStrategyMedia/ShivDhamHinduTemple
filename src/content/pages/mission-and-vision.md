## Our Mission

Shri Shiv Dham Hindu Temple exists to provide a place of worship and study, of Sanatan Dharma, Hindu philosophy, and the Vedic way of life, open to all, without regard to caste, creed, color, nationality, ethnicity, gender, or religion.

## Our Vision

The temple works to establish and sustain religious, cultural, spiritual, and educational activity for the Hindu community in and around Orlando, and for anyone drawn to learn about Hinduism.

## Sustained by Community

The temple's programs, priests, and facilities are sustained entirely by the generosity of the community it serves. Every [donation](/online-donations/), large or small, goes directly toward keeping worship, festivals, and classes open to all who seek them.
