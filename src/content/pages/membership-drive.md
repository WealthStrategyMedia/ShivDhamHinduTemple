Shri Shiv Dham Hindu Temple members and their families are entitled to the temple's full religious privileges, along with voting rights to elect the temple's Executive Committee and Board of Directors members.

Annual membership is open to any individual who donates a minimum of **$365** within the temple's membership cycle. Any receipted payment made to the temple toward services or donations counts toward membership; cash donations unfortunately cannot be counted, due to anonymity, so the temple encourages giving by check or card.

Renewing or starting your membership is a way to make sure your voice, and your family's, is heard in the decisions that shape the temple's future.

## Membership Includes

- A yearly temple calendar
- Archana performed in your name on the first and last Monday of every month, for all 12 months, with Sankalpa

## How to Join

Contact the temple office to start or renew your annual membership, or make a $365+ donation through the form below and note "Annual Membership" in your message.
