This policy covers the information collected through shivatempleorlando.org, the official website of Shri Shiv Dham Hindu Temple.

## Information We Collect

- **Contact and booking forms.** When you submit the contact form, a puja booking request, or a similar form, we collect the name, email, phone number, and message you provide, so the temple office can respond to you.
- **Donations.** Online donations are processed securely by Stripe, our payment processor. We do not receive or store your full card details; Stripe handles that directly under its own privacy and security practices.
- **Basic site analytics.** We may use standard, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how the site is used, such as which pages are visited, so we can keep improving it. This data is aggregated and not used to identify individual visitors.

## How We Use It

Information submitted through this site is used only to respond to your inquiry, process your donation or booking request, and keep you informed about temple events if you've asked to hear from us. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

## Third-Party Services

The site relies on a small number of trusted service providers to function: Tweeble (the temple's community hub) for donations, volunteer sign-ups, and contact-form submissions, Stripe for payment processing on the Temple Store, and Netlify for hosting. Each operates under its own privacy policy governing the data it processes on our behalf.

## Your Choices

You're welcome to contact the temple office at any time to ask what information we hold about you, or to request that it be corrected or removed.

## Questions

For any questions about this policy, [contact the temple office](/contact-us/) directly.
