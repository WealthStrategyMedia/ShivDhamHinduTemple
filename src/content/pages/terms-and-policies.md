Welcome to Shri Shiv Dham Hindu Temple. By visiting and using the temple's premises, website, or participating in any of its services, you agree to comply with the following terms and policies.

## Temple Use and Conduct

- All visitors must respect the sanctity of the temple and maintain decorum while inside the premises.
- Offensive behavior, disruptive activities, or any action that could disturb other visitors or devotees is strictly prohibited.
- Shoes must be removed before entering the prayer halls.
- Use of mobile phones or other electronic devices should be limited and kept on silent mode during religious services.

## Event Participation

- Participation in religious events, ceremonies, or festivals is open to all, provided visitors follow temple guidelines.
- Large groups or special event participation may require prior registration or notification to temple management.
- The temple reserves the right to modify, reschedule, or cancel events due to unforeseen circumstances.

## Donations and Offerings

- Donations to the temple are voluntary. Offerings made at the temple or through the temple's website support its maintenance, development, and charitable activities.
- All donations are non-refundable. Donation receipts are issued for tax purposes as required by applicable law.
- Shri Shiv Dham Hindu Temple is committed to transparency in the use of donations.

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties.
