The temple canteen serves prasad and meals to devotees on puja days and throughout festival celebrations, run by a dedicated team of volunteers. It's as much a place of community as it is a place to eat: many devotees linger after worship to share a meal.

Interested in volunteering with the canteen team, or in contributing culinary offerings for an upcoming event? See [Volunteering](/volunteering/) and [Culinary Contributions](/culinary-contributions/) to get involved.
