Hiranya Shraddha is performed to honor departed ancestors, offering remembrance and gratitude on their behalf, typically on their death anniversary (tithi) or during Pitru Paksha, the annual fortnight set aside for ancestral worship. The priest performs the ritual with rice, sesame, and other traditional offerings, along with mantras invoking peace for the departed soul.

The "hiranya" form refers to a simplified version of the ceremony performed with a symbolic monetary offering in place of the more elaborate full-day Shraddha ritual, making it more accessible while still fulfilling the family's obligation of remembrance.

Performed at the temple or in your home, guided by the priest through each step.
