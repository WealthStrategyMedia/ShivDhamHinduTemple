Satyanarayana Pooja is a worship of Lord Vishnu, performed both to express gratitude for a wish fulfilled and to seek continued blessings on a family or undertaking. The ceremony includes the reading of the Satyanarayana Vratha Katha, a set of devotional stories illustrating the rewards of faith and the consequences of neglecting a promised vow.

It's one of the most frequently sponsored ceremonies in Hindu households, often performed to mark a new job, a graduation, the purchase of a home, or simply as a periodic renewal of devotion. Families commonly gather friends and relatives for the reading and the prasad that follows.

Performed at the temple or in your home, with the full katha reading guided by the priest.
