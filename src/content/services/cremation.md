Antyesti, the Hindu rite of passing, is performed to honor the departed and support their soul's journey. The temple's priests guide families through the rituals with patience and care, from the final rites through the Shraddha ceremonies that follow in the weeks and months after.

Every family's circumstances are different, and this is a service the temple prefers to arrange through a direct conversation rather than an online booking. Please [contact the temple office](/contact-us/) and a member of our team will reach out promptly to support your family.
