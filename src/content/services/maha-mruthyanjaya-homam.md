Maha Mrityunjaya Homam centers on the Mahamrityunjaya Mantra, one of the most revered mantras in Hindu tradition, addressed to Lord Shiva as the conqueror of death and the source of healing. The priest offers into the sacred fire while chanting the mantra, invoking protection from illness, accident, and untimely death.

This homam is one of the temple's most requested remedial rituals, frequently sponsored during serious illness, before surgery, or as an ongoing protective measure for someone facing a difficult health situation.

Performed at the temple or in your home, with the mantra recited throughout the offering.
