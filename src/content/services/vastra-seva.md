Vastra Seva is an offering of new garments to a deity, an act of devotion rooted in caring for the murti as one would care for an honored guest in one's own home. The priest dresses the deity in the new garments as part of the day's worship, often alongside flowers and other adornments.

It's a favored offering for special occasions, a festival, an anniversary, or simply an act of gratitude, and sponsors are welcome to specify a color or fabric with particular meaning to their family.

Choose the deity you'd like to sponsor garments for, and the temple's priests will handle the rest.
