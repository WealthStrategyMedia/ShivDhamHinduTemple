This combined offering pairs Rudra Homam with Rudra Abhishekam, giving sponsors the full weight of both a fire ritual and a ceremonial bathing dedicated to Lord Shiva in a single sponsorship. The priest performs the homam first, then continues directly into the lingam's abhishek, both accompanied by verses from the Rudram.

Devotees seeking the most complete form of Shiva worship the temple offers often choose this combined option, particularly around Pradosham or Maha Shivratri.

Performed at the temple's Shiva shrine by the resident priests.
