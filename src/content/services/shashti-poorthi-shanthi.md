Shashti Poorthi Shanthi marks the completion of sixty years of life, a milestone considered highly significant in Vedic astrology, since it marks the completion of a full cycle of the sixty-year Hindu calendar. The ceremony seeks blessings for a healthy, peaceful, and fulfilling continuation of life beyond this milestone.

Family members often use the occasion to renew wedding vows or simply gather to honor the person reaching this milestone, making it as much a family celebration as a devotional one.

Performed at the temple or in your home, with the full ritual guided by the priest.
