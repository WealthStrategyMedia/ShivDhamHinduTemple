Rudra Abhishekam is a ceremonial bathing of the Shiva lingam, performed with milk, water, honey, yogurt, and other sacred substances while the priest recites verses from the Rudram, a hymn from the Yajur Veda dedicated to Lord Shiva in his fierce, transformative aspect.

It's consistently one of the temple's most requested offerings, drawing devotees seeking relief from difficulty, protection from harm, or simply a deeper connection with Shiva's grace. Many devotees time this offering around Pradosham or Maha Shivratri, though it can be sponsored on any day.

Performed at the temple's Shiva shrine by the resident priests.
