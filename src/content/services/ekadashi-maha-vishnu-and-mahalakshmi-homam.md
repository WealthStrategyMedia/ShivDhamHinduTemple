This homam honors Lord Vishnu and Goddess Mahalakshmi together, offered on Ekadashi, the eleventh day of each lunar fortnight and one of the most spiritually significant days in the Hindu calendar. The priest performs the fire ritual while invoking both deities for prosperity, harmony, and spiritual merit.

Ekadashi is widely observed through fasting and focused devotion, and sponsoring this homam is a way to deepen that observance with an active offering rather than fasting alone.

Performed at the temple by the resident priests; see the [Events Calendar](/events/) for the current lunar month's Ekadashi date.
