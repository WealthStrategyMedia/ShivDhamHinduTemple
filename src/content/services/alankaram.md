Alankaram is the ceremonial dressing and adornment of a murti, with fresh garments, flowers, and ornaments arranged by the priest as an offering of beauty and devotion. It's often performed on a special day, such as a birthday, anniversary, or in thanksgiving for a wish fulfilled.

Sponsoring an Alankaram is considered a meaningful way to honor a deity directly, since the sponsor's care and intention are reflected in how the murti is presented to the whole congregation that day.

Choose the deity you'd like adorned when you book, and the temple's priests will take care of the rest.
