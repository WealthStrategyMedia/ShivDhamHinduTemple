This is the fuller version of the temple's housewarming service, combining Gruha Pravesham with a Satyanarayana Vratha Katha reading performed in the new home. Alongside the entrance havan and purification rituals, the priest recites the story of Satyanarayana Vratha, a devotional narrative offered in thanksgiving and to invite Lord Vishnu's blessing on the household.

Families often choose this extended version when they want the housewarming to double as an occasion to gather relatives and friends for a fuller day of worship, storytelling, and prasad.

The temple's priests travel to your home to perform the complete ceremony. Contact the temple office to arrange a date.
