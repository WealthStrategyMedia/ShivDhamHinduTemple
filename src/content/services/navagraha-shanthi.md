Navagraha Shanthi is a remedial ritual addressing the nine planetary deities of Vedic astrology together, performed to ease a difficult planetary period and restore balance in a devotee's life. The priest performs puja at the temple's Navagraha shrine, invoking each planetary deity in turn.

It's most often sponsored during a challenging astrological transit, such as Sade Sati (Saturn's extended influence) or any period a family's astrologer has flagged as difficult, though many devotees also sponsor it simply as a periodic act of protection.

Performed at the temple's Navagraha shrine by the resident priests. See [Navagraha Shanthi Home](/puja-bookings/navagraha-shanthi-home/) for the version performed at your residence.
