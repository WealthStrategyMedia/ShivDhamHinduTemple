Shravan Mela is the temple's community fair held during the auspicious month of Shravan, drawing vendors offering food, clothing, jewelry, and devotional items to a large gathering of devotees. Registering a booth is a way for local businesses and artisans to reach the temple's community while supporting the festival.

Category 1 includes standard booth space for the day. A larger Category 2 booth is also available. Reach out to the temple office for setup times and any additional requirements.
