Akshara Abhyasam marks a child's first formal introduction to letters, typically performed around the age of three, on Vijayadashami, or on another auspicious day chosen by the family. Under priestly guidance, the child traces their first letters, often in a tray of rice or sand, while the priest invokes Goddess Saraswati's blessing for a lifetime of learning.

The ceremony is simple but deeply symbolic: it treats a child's education as something sacred from its very first step, not just a milestone to check off. Many families choose to combine it with a small puja to Ganesha, invoked first for a smooth beginning to any new undertaking.

Performed at the temple's shrine or in your home, with the priest guiding both child and parents through each step.
