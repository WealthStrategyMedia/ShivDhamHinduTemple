Rudra Homam is a fire ritual dedicated to Lord Shiva as Rudra, his fierce, transformative aspect, invoked to burn away obstacles, negativity, and difficulty at their root. The priest offers sacred substances into the fire while reciting verses from the Rudram, one of the most powerful hymns in Shiva worship.

It's frequently sponsored during periods of hardship, before a major life change, or as part of the temple's larger Maha Shivratri observances, when devotion to Shiva reaches its peak for the year.

Performed at the temple or in your home, with the full ritual guided by the priest from start to finish.
