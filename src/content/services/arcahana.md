Archana is one of the simplest and most personal forms of worship: the priest recites the names of the sponsor and their family alongside the sacred names of the deity, offering flowers or rice as each name is called. It's typically performed for a specific intention: gratitude, a request for wellbeing, or simply a regular devotional habit.

Because it's brief and affordable, many devotees sponsor an Archana on nearly every temple visit, treating it as a small, consistent thread of devotion woven through daily or weekly life.

Performed at the temple's shrine; let the priest know the names and the deity you'd like the offering directed to.
