Mundan Sanskar is a child's ceremonial first haircut, usually performed between the age of one and three. The ritual shaving symbolizes the release of any negativity carried over from past lives and marks a fresh, purified start as the child grows.

The priest performs a short puja before the haircut itself, invoking blessings for the child's health and long life. Families often keep the day light and celebratory, with grandparents and relatives present to mark the milestone together.

Performed at the temple or in your home, whichever setting your family is most comfortable with.
