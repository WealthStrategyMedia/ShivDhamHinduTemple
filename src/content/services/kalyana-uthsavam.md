Kalyana Uthsavam re-enacts the divine marriage of a deity, most often Venkateswara and Padmavati, as a devotional offering rather than a family life-cycle rite. Sponsoring the ceremony is considered highly auspicious, believed to bring the same blessings associated with witnessing or supporting a sacred union.

The priest conducts the full wedding ritual before the murtis, complete with mangalsutra, garlands, and the mantras of a real marriage ceremony, while sponsors and devotees look on. Many families sponsor Kalyana Uthsavam to mark an anniversary, seek blessings for an unmarried family member, or simply as an act of devotion.

Available at the temple's shrine or, for a smaller home version, in your own puja space.
