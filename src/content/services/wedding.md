A Hindu wedding is one of the most elaborate and meaningful of the life-cycle samskaras, uniting two families before Agni, the sacred fire, as witness to lifelong vows. The priest guides the couple through each stage of the ceremony, including Kanyadaan (the giving away of the bride), Panigrahana (the joining of hands), and Saptapadi (the seven steps taken together around the fire, each one a vow for married life).

Every family brings its own regional customs and preferences to the day, and the temple's priests are experienced in adapting the ceremony's structure, length, and language to match what matters most to you, while keeping the core Vedic rites intact.

Available at the temple's shrine hall or at your own wedding venue. Contact the temple office to discuss your date, the ceremony's structure, and any regional customs you'd like included.
