Namakaranam is the traditional naming ceremony for a newborn, typically performed on the eleventh or twelfth day after birth, though many families schedule it whenever it best suits the baby's health and the family's readiness. The priest determines an auspicious name based on the child's nakshatra (birth star) and rasi (moon sign), and whispers it into the baby's ear as part of the ritual.

The ceremony includes a short puja invoking a long, healthy life for the child, and is often the newborn's first formal introduction to the family's extended circle and community.

Performed at the temple or in the comfort of your own home.
