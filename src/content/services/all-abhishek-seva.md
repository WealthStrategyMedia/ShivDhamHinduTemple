All Abhishek Seva extends the ceremonial bathing offering across every murti enshrined at the temple in one sponsorship, rather than selecting a single deity. The priest performs abhishek at each shrine in turn, so the sponsor's intention reaches the full pantheon honored at Shri Shiv Dham.

It's a popular choice for devotees who want to mark a significant occasion, such as a birthday or anniversary, with the broadest possible blessing, or who simply feel drawn to honor all seven deities together rather than choosing among them.

Performed at the temple's shrines by the resident priests.
