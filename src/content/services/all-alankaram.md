All Alankaram extends the deity's ceremonial dressing and adornment across every murti enshrined at the temple, rather than a single shrine. Each deity is freshly garmented, garlanded, and adorned as part of one coordinated sponsorship, and the effect is felt across the whole temple that day.

Families often choose this offering for a major occasion, an anniversary, a wish fulfilled, or simply a desire to honor the full pantheon at once, since it's among the most complete expressions of devotion the temple offers.

Performed at the temple's shrines by the resident priests.
