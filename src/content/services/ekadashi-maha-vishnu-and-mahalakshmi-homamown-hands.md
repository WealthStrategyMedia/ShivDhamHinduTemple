This is the participatory version of the temple's Ekadashi Maha Vishnu and Mahalakshmi Homam. Rather than the priest performing every offering on the sponsor's behalf, the sponsor sits with the priest and personally places offerings into the sacred fire, guided step by step through the ritual.

For many devotees, offering with their own hands makes the experience feel more direct and personal, an active participation in the ritual rather than an offering made only on their behalf.

Performed at the temple with a priest guiding the sponsor through each offering; see the [Events Calendar](/events/) for the current Ekadashi date.
