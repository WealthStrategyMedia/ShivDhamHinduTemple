This is the home version of Navagraha Shanthi, the remedial ritual addressing all nine planetary deities of Vedic astrology, performed in your own puja space rather than at the temple. The priest brings everything needed to invoke each planetary deity and restore balance during a difficult astrological period.

Many families prefer the home setting for remedial rituals like this one, allowing the whole household to participate together in a familiar space.

The temple's priests travel to your home to perform the complete ritual. See [Navagraha Shanthi](/puja-bookings/navagraha-shanthi/) for the temple-shrine version.
