Sudharshana Homam invokes the Sudarshana Chakra, Lord Vishnu's discus, revered as a powerful protective force against negative energy, ill will, and obstacles caused by others. The priest offers into the fire while reciting mantras associated with the Sudarshana form of Vishnu.

Families often sponsor this homam during a period of unexplained difficulty, before a significant undertaking, or as a periodic protective measure for the household.

Performed at the temple or in your home, with the full ritual guided by the priest.
