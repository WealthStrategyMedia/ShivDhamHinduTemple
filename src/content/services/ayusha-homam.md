Ayusha Homam is a Vedic fire ritual performed to invoke long life, good health, and vitality, most often sponsored around a birthday or after a period of illness. The priest offers sacred herbs, ghee, and grains into the consecrated fire while reciting mantras dedicated to longevity and wellbeing.

Families frequently choose this homam for a child's birthday, an elder's milestone year, or simply as a periodic renewal of health and protection for the household.

Performed at the temple or, with proper arrangements, in your home.
