Ganesha Homam invokes Lord Ganesha, remover of obstacles, before any significant undertaking: a new job, a business launch, a move, or the start of an important project. Tradition holds that beginning with Ganesha's blessing clears the path of hidden difficulties before they arise.

The priest offers grains, ghee, and sacred herbs into the fire while reciting mantras dedicated to Ganesha, closing with a short puja invoking wisdom and a smooth beginning.

Performed at the temple or in your home, ideally scheduled just before the undertaking it's meant to bless.
