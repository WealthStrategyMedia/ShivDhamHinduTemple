Bilva Seva is an offering of trifoliate bilva (bael) leaves to the Shiva lingam, one of the most cherished offerings in Shiva worship. Tradition holds that even a single bilva leaf, offered with sincere devotion, is deeply pleasing to Lord Shiva.

The priest offers the leaves along with mantras from the Bilvashtakam, the traditional eight-verse hymn dedicated to this exact offering. It's a favored choice on Mondays, during Pradosham, and especially on Maha Shivratri, when devotion to Shiva is at its peak.

Performed at the temple's Shiva shrine by the resident priests.
