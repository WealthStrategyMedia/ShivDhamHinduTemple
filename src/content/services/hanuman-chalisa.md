This listing lets you sponsor a group recitation of the Hanuman Chalisa performed at the temple, dedicating the merit of that day's recitation to your intention, whether for strength, protection, or the resolution of a specific difficulty.

The Hanuman Chalisa is one of the most widely recited devotional hymns in Hinduism, and a group recitation carries a collective energy many devotees find especially powerful. Sponsors are welcome to attend and join the recitation in person.

See the [108 Hanuman Chalisa](/events/108-hanuman-chalisa/) event page for the temple's larger recurring group recitation, or sponsor an individual session through this listing.
