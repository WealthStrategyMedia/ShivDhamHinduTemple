Gruha Pravesham is performed when a family moves into a new home, inviting divine blessing before daily life begins under a new roof. The priest performs havan at the entrance and throughout the home, purifying the space and welcoming Lord Ganesha, remover of obstacles, along with Lakshmi, goddess of prosperity, to reside there.

Vastu tradition holds that a home's earliest energy sets the tone for everything that follows within it, so many families choose to have Gruha Pravesham performed before moving in any furniture, or as close to move-in day as possible.

Because the ritual centers on your specific home, the temple's priests travel to perform it on-site. Contact the temple office to arrange a date and discuss any preparation needed beforehand.
