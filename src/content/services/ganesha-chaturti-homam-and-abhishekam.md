This combined offering pairs a Ganesha Homam with an Abhishekam, performed together in honor of Lord Ganesha on Chaturthi, the fourth day of the lunar fortnight associated with him. The priest conducts the fire ritual first, then follows with the ceremonial bathing of the murti.

Sponsoring both together is a way of offering a fuller expression of devotion to Ganesha, remover of obstacles, on the day of the month most closely tied to his worship.

Performed at the temple by the resident priests; see the [Events Calendar](/events/) for the next Sankata Hara Chaturthi date.
