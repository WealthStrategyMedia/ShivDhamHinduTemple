Abhishek is the ceremonial bathing of a murti, using substances such as milk, water, honey, and sacred ash, each carrying its own devotional meaning. The priest performs the ritual with mantras invoking the deity's blessing, while the sponsor's name is offered as part of the prayer.

It's one of the most common ways devotees express gratitude or seek a specific blessing, whether for health, a family matter, or simply as a regular act of devotion. Choose the deity you'd like the abhishek performed for when you book.

Performed at the temple's shrine by the resident priests.
