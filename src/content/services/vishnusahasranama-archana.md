This offering sponsors a recitation of the Vishnu Sahasranama, the thousand sacred names of Lord Vishnu, performed as archana with the sponsor's name offered alongside each verse. It's considered one of the most powerful devotional recitations in Vaishnava tradition, believed to bring peace, protection, and spiritual merit to those who sponsor or recite it.

Devotees often choose this offering during times of difficulty or transition, or simply as a regular devotional practice tied to Lord Vishnu specifically.

Performed at the temple's shrine by the resident priests.
