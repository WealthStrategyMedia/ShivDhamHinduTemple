This is a shorter form of Rudra Abhishekam, built around a single (eka) complete recitation of the Rudram during the lingam's ceremonial bathing, rather than the extended multi-recitation version. It's a meaningful, more accessible way to sponsor the same core ritual without committing to the fuller ceremony.

Many devotees choose this version for a specific, focused intention, marking a particular day or need, while still receiving the full weight of the Rudram's verses recited over the abhishek.

Available at the temple or, for a home version, in your own puja space.
