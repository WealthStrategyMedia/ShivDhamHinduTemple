Mukhya Yajamana is the primary sponsorship role for one of the temple's major festivals, traditionally held by the family that bears the greatest share of a celebration's cost and, by tradition, receives the honor of leading its rituals. The Mukhya Yajamana typically sits closest to the priest during key ceremonies and is recognized publicly throughout the festival.

Sponsoring at this level is a significant act of generosity that sustains the temple's largest celebrations, and many families consider it an honor passed down across generations, sponsoring the same festival year after year.

Contact the temple office directly to discuss which upcoming festival you'd like to sponsor and what the role involves.
