Navagraha Abhishekam is a ceremonial bathing of the nine planetary murtis at the temple's Navagraha shrine, performed with milk, water, and other sacred substances as the priest recites planetary mantras for each. It's a gentler, more accessible alternative to a full Navagraha Shanthi for devotees who want to honor the planetary deities without the fuller remedial ritual.

Many devotees sponsor this abhishekam as a regular devotional practice, or to mark a specific astrologically significant day.

Performed at the temple's Navagraha shrine by the resident priests.
