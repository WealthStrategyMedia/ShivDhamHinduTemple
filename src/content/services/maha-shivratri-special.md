This offering sponsors a devotional archana performed during the temple's Maha Shivratri celebration, the night-long observance marking the anniversary of the temple's grand opening and the year's most significant worship of Lord Shiva.

Sponsoring an offering on this particular night is considered especially auspicious, since Maha Shivratri is traditionally seen as the year's most powerful occasion for connecting with Shiva's grace.

See the [Maha Shivratri](/events/maha-shivratri/) event page for the full night's schedule, and sponsor your offering through this listing.
