Bhoomi Pooja is performed at the construction site before groundbreaking begins, asking forgiveness of Bhoomi Devi, the earth goddess, for disturbing the land and inviting her blessing on the project ahead. The priest performs a havan and offers prayers for a safe, smooth construction process.

It's traditionally performed on a home, a business, or any significant new building, and many families consider it as essential a first step as breaking ground itself.

Since the ritual is tied to a specific site, the temple's priests travel to perform it there. Contact the temple office to arrange a date.
