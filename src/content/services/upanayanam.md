Upanayanam, the sacred thread ceremony, formally marks a young person's entry into Vedic study, traditionally performed between the ages of seven and twelve. The priest invests the initiate with the sacred thread (yajnopavita) and teaches the Gayatri Mantra, the foundational verse recited daily from that point forward.

The ceremony is one of the more elaborate life-cycle rites, often spanning a full day of ritual, including a homam and blessings from family elders. It's traditionally seen as a "second birth," marking the beginning of a young person's formal spiritual and intellectual life.

Performed at the temple or in your home, with the full ritual sequence guided by the priest from start to finish.
