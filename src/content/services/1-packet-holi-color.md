A single packet of vibrant color powder, sold at the temple during its annual Holi celebration. Splashing color is the heart of Holi, the festival marking the arrival of spring and the triumph of good over evil, and every packet purchased helps the temple cover the cost of hosting the celebration for the whole community.

Pick up a packet at the temple during the event, or arrange one in advance through the temple office.
