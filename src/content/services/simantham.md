Simantham, also known as Seemantham or Godh Bharai in some regions, is performed during pregnancy, typically in the seventh or eighth month, to bless both mother and child and ease the transition toward childbirth. The priest performs a puja invoking protection and a smooth delivery, and family members offer their blessings to the expectant mother.

The ceremony is often a warm, celebratory occasion, giving the extended family and friends a meaningful way to gather and shower the mother with support before the baby arrives.

Available at the temple or in your home, whichever is more comfortable for the mother.
