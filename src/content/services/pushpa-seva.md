Pushpa Seva is a floral offering, decorating the murti with fresh flowers as an expression of devotion and gratitude. Flowers hold a special place in Hindu worship: fragrant, beautiful, and impermanent, they're seen as a fitting offering for something as fleeting and precious as a single act of devotion.

The priest arranges the flowers as part of the deity's daily adornment, and sponsors are welcome to specify a particular occasion the offering is meant to mark, an anniversary, thanksgiving, or simply an ongoing devotional practice.

Choose the deity you'd like honored, and the temple's priests will take care of the arrangement.
