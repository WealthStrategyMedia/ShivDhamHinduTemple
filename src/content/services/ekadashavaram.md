Ekadasha Varam is a sponsorship covering eleven consecutive worship offerings performed on the sponsor's behalf, rather than a single one-time ritual. Committing to a repeated offering across eleven occasions is considered a way of deepening devotion through consistency, echoing the significance the number eleven carries in several Vedic worship traditions.

Families often choose this option when praying for a sustained outcome, such as recovery from illness or resolution of an ongoing family matter, where a single offering feels too brief for the intention behind it.

Available at the temple or, for a home version, in your own puja space; contact the temple office to schedule the sequence of offerings.
