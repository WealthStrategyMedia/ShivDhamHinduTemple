As the temple's programs have grown — Bal Vihar, Sadvidya Pathshala, language and music classes, and the annual summer camp — so has the need for a real administrative home base and a proper space for educational materials. This project creates a dedicated administrative office, along with a books and library space to support the temple's education programs and children's camps.

## How This Helps

**The temple:** A proper office gives temple staff and volunteers a real place to manage bookings, records, and programs, instead of working out of borrowed space. It's the quiet infrastructure that lets everything else — from puja bookings to festival planning — run smoothly.

**The immediate community:** Families whose children attend Bal Vihar, Sadvidya Pathshala, or summer camp benefit directly from a real library and dedicated teaching space, giving children a proper environment to learn scripture, language, and values rather than making do with shared or temporary rooms.

**The wider community:** A stronger educational foundation helps the temple raise the next generation of community leaders and volunteers, and a real library becomes a resource the whole Central Florida Hindu community can draw on for learning about scripture, culture, and tradition.
