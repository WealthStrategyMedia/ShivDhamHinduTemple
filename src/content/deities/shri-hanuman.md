**Shri Hanuman** is revered for his unwavering devotion to Shri Ram, whom he served with complete humility and selfless dedication. Depicted with one palm raised in blessing, he is recognized instantly by his monkey-like features and his posture of readiness to serve.

Devotees turn to **Hanuman** for the mental and physical strength to face life's challenges, and for the example he sets of courage paired with humility. His life is a model of selfless service, a devotee who found the divine not by seeking personal glory, but through complete surrender to another.

The temple honors him with regular recitation of the Hanuman Chalisa, including the recurring 108 Hanuman Chalisa observance, and celebrates his birth each year at Hanuman Jayanti.
