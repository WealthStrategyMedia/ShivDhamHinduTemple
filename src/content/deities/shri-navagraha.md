The **Navagraha** are the nine celestial forces of Vedic astrology, each governing a distinct dimension of life and believed to shape one's destiny. Worshipping them together is a way of seeking balance among the many forces that influence a person's path, and of restoring peace and prosperity where they have fallen out of harmony.

- **Surya (Sun):** vitality, leadership, and self-esteem
- **Chandra (Moon):** emotion, mind, and inner peace
- **Mangala (Mars):** strength, courage, and ambition
- **Budha (Mercury):** intelligence, communication, and adaptability
- **Guru (Jupiter):** wisdom, spirituality, and growth
- **Shukra (Venus):** love, beauty, and artistic expression
- **Shani (Saturn):** discipline, perseverance, and patience
- **Rahu:** desire, ambition, and material pursuit
- **Ketu:** detachment, spirituality, and liberation

Devotees seeking to ease difficult planetary influences or restore balance in their lives can arrange a [Navagraha Shanthi puja](/puja-bookings/) at the temple.
